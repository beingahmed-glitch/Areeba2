Aapki aankhon ke naam 🎀

Aap itni khoobsoorat hain ke aapko dekhne ke liye
aankhein udhaar li jaa sakti hain. 🎀

Sadiyan guzaari jaa sakti hain, 🎀
waqt ki raftaar ghataai jaa sakti hai, 🎀
phoolon ki khoobsoorti ko madham kiya jaa sakta hai. 🎀

Aapki aankhon mein qaus-e-quzah se zyada rang chhupe hain, 🎀
jaam se zyada nasha hai. 🎀

Khuda ki qudrat ki sabse khoobsurat cheez bayan karne ka mauqa mile,
to main aapki aankhein bayan karoon ga. 🎀

Aapki aankhein aisi hain ke poore adab mein
aapki aankhon par likha jaa sakta hai. 🎀

main apni tamaam chaar din ki umr daraz guzaar sakta hoon. 🎀

Aapki aankhon pe likhte hue apne baalon ki safedi dekh sakta hoon. 🎀

Aapki aankhein dekhte dekhte apni beenaai se mehroom ho sakta hoon. 🎀
